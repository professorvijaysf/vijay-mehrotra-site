About

I'm a Full Professor of Business Analytics at the University of San Francisco, with a PhD in Operations Research from Stanford. For more than 30 years I've written an Analytics column, and I host The Decision Intelligence Lab podcast. I'm currently writing a book, Capturing Value From Decision Intelligence Technologies, for Springer.

Outside of the classroom, I consult and advise, invest, travel, write, and occasionally act. This site is a "let it all hang out" home for all of it — the professional and the personal.
